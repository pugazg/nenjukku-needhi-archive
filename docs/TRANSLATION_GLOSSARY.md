# Nenjukku Needhi — Translation Glossary

This is a living glossary. Entries are added only after reading the Tamil in context. No term is mechanically frozen merely because it has appeared once.

## Principles

- Preserve political and emotional force.
- Prefer Kalaignar's relationship to a person or idea over detached encyclopedic naming when the Tamil uses an epithet/form of address.
- Transliteration, translation and first-use glossing are all available; choose according to context.
- Do not silently replace Kalaignar's Tamil rendering of a quotation with an external canonical English version.
- Record meaningful changes of decision here so later chapters remain consistent.
- Historical/dialectal Tamil must be resolved from speaker and context before a modern surface meaning is chosen.
- A culturally specific gesture or formula may remain transliterated with a minimal functional gloss when a generic English replacement would erase what the Tamil is doing.
- Historical proper names and institutional titles may be normalised to their securely attested English forms when literal translation would misidentify the entity; such normalisation must be disclosed in source notes.
