## Audit principles

This gate checks historically useful proper-name forms, recurring political vocabulary, culturally specific epithets, movement terminology and lexical choices against the living glossary and the chapter's own context. It does not silently modernize Kalaignar's ideology or replace his rhetoric with contemporary neutral language.

A term is frozen globally only when the evidence supports that scope. Chapter-specific decisions remain chapter-specific.

## Findings

### 1. `தமிழர் படை`

The identifiable 1938 marching body is already rendered **Tamil Brigade**. Historical English summaries also gloss `Tamilar Padai` as Tamil Brigade.

**Action:** retain **Tamil Brigade** for this event. Do not generalize the choice to every future `படை`.

### 2. Pattukkottai Alagirisami / Alagiri

The Tamil source first gives `பட்டுக்கோட்டை அழகிரிசாமி` and later uses `அழகிரி`. Historical English sources vary between Alagiri / Alagirisami / Azhagirisamy.

**Action:** retain the source-led alternation **Pattukkottai Alagirisami** → **Alagiri**. Do not impose a speculative corpus-wide romanisation from this chapter alone.

### 3. `அஞ்சா நெஞ்சன்`

This is an active praise-name and contributes to the portrait.

**Action:** retain **“Fearless Heart”**. Do not flatten to “fearless man” or delete it as an honorific.

### 4. `கோடையிடி அழகிரி`

The epithet is part of the chapter's sound-and-weather rhetoric and is later echoed by `கோடை இடி குமுறியது`.

**Action:** retain **Summer-Thunder Alagiri**. The unusual English is intentional because the repeated thunder image is active in the Tamil.

### 5. Moovalur Ramamirtham Ammaiyar

`அம்மையார்` is culturally meaningful respectful address, and this project already preserves such honorifics when they carry voice rather than acting as detachable metadata.

**Action:** retain **Moovalur Ramamirtham Ammaiyar** on first naming and **Moovalur Ammaiyar** thereafter.

### 6. Manavai Thirumalaisami / `நகரதூதன்` / `பேனா நர்த்தனம்`

The person and publication/title are not secure candidates for aggressive English normalization from available evidence.

**Action:** retain **Manavai Thirumalaisami**, **Nagarathoothan**, and translated column/title **“Pen Dance.”** Do not invent an English publication title.

### 7. `சுயமரியாதை இயக்கம்` / `தன்மான இயக்கம்`

Both expressions refer here to the same Self-Respect political current.

**Action:** retain **Self-Respect Movement** for both in this context. This is semantic continuity, not erasure of a source distinction in ideology.

### 8. `வெறி / பற்று`

The lexical opposition is itself Kalaignar's argument.

**Action:** retain **frenzy / devotion**. Do not replace it with softer modern language such as “enthusiasm / affection.”

### 9. `மறியல் அறப்போர்`

The T2 rendering preserves both components: picketing plus a non-violent/moral struggle register.

**Action:** retain **daily picketing as a non-violent struggle** for this occurrence. Do not freeze `அறப்போர்` to one English expression across all future contexts.

### 10. `இன உணர்ச்சி`

T2 retained provisional **sense of race** pending this gate. The phrase belongs to the period's political language, and historical Tamil-English usage supports **race consciousness** / **racial consciousness** for `இன உணர்வு`.

“Sense of race” is awkward English and weakens the ideological noun `உணர்ச்சி`.

**Revision 1:** in Anna's quotation, use **our racial consciousness to well up**.

Scope rule: this is a **chapter-specific historical-register decision**. It does not create a global one-to-one translation for `இனம்`, `இன உணர்வு`, `இன உணர்ச்சி` or `தமிழினம்`.

### 11. `தமிழ்க் காளையர்`

T2/T3 provisionally kept **two Tamil bulls** to avoid prematurely softening the source. Lexical review shows `காளை` also has established human senses: **young man in his prime**, **man**, and **warrior**. In this sentence, the noun is applied directly to Thalamuthu and Natarajan; a literal English “bulls” risks manufacturing an animal metaphor where Tamil can function as an honorific heroic noun.

**Revision 2:** use **the two young Tamil warriors Thalamuthu and Natarajan became corpses in prison**.

This preserves youth/heroic force and the source's blunt `பிணமாயினர்` without over-literalizing `காளையர்`.

### 12. N. V. Natarajan / Thalamuthu / Natarajan

Retain **N. V. Natarajan** for the Congress-associated activist and **Natarajan** for the prison-death name, following the project's established English form. Do not infer that every Tamil `நடராசன்` across the corpus refers to the same person.

### 13. Gandhiji

The Tamil uses `காந்தியார்`, carrying respectful register.

**Action:** retain **Gandhiji** in the final political widening, consistent with the project's treatment of reverential Gandhi forms where English can carry them naturally.

### 14. `பச்சைப் பிறைக் கொடி`

The closing image coordinates `தமிழ்க் கொடி` with `பச்சைப் பிறைக் கொடி`.

**Action:** retain **the Tamil flag and the green crescent flag flying together**. Do not replace the second image with an explanatory party label; the visual pairing is Kalaignar's closing rhetorical action.

### 15. English title

`தமிழ் காக்கும் போர் முனை` remains **The Battlefront to Save Tamil**.

**Action:** retain. The title's battlefront image is active throughout the chapter and should not be flattened to “Anti-Hindi Agitation.”
