## Audit principles

This gate checks publication titles and forms, political entity names, honorific variation, culturally specific publishing vocabulary, movement terminology, slogans and proper-name consistency against the living glossary and the chapter's own Tamil context.

It does not force one English form where Kalaignar deliberately varies honorifics or name forms, and it does not use historical normalization as a pretext to repair authored chronology.

## Findings

### 1. `மாணவ நேசன்`

The title is already retained as **Maanava Nesan**, visibly marked as a publication rather than flattened to “student magazine.”

**Action:** retain **Maanava Nesan** as the chapter's working romanized publication title.

Scope rule: this confirms the chapter-level form. It does **not** impose a final corpus-wide romanisation system before wider evidence is reviewed.

### 2. Title question versus visitor's question

The chapter title and the visitor's in-scene wording are not identical in Tamil.

**Action:** retain the distinction between **Are You the One Who Runs 'Maanava Nesan'?** and **Are you Karunanidhi, the one who runs 'Maanava Nesan'?**

Do not regularize them into the same English formula merely for consistency.

### 3. Jinnah honorific variation

The source alternates ordinary Jinnah references with `ஜனாப் ஜின்னா`.

**Action:** retain **Jinnah** where the source is plain and **Janab Jinnah** where the honorific is present.

Do not globally prefix every Jinnah occurrence with **Janab**.

### 4. Gandhi honorific variation

The Tamil alternates `காந்தியடிகள்`, `காந்தியார்` and `மகாத்மா காந்தி`.

The English correspondingly uses **Gandhiji**, **Mahatma Gandhi** and, where syntax naturally requires, **Gandhi** in a relational phrase such as the Gandhi–Subhas relationship.

**Action:** retain the source-led variation. Do not flatten all forms to bare **Gandhi** or force **Gandhiji** into every occurrence.

### 5. Nehru forms

The source uses Jawaharlal Nehru and `பண்டித நேரு` in different places.

**Action:** retain **Jawaharlal Nehru** / **Nehru** contextually and **Pandit Nehru** where the source explicitly uses the honorific.

### 6. Indian National Congress / Congress Party

`அகில இந்திய காங்கிரஸ் கட்சி` has already been normalized to **Indian National Congress**, while ordinary `காங்கிரஸ் கட்சி` references remain **Congress Party** or **Congress** according to syntax.

**Action:** retain this distinction. Historical proper-name normalization should not mechanically convert every ordinary Congress reference into the full formal name.

### 7. Muslim League / Justice Party / Dravidian movement

**Muslim League** and **Justice Party** are established historical English names. **Dravidian movement** is transparent in this chapter and remains lower-case as a movement description rather than being expanded into an unsupported institutional name.

**Action:** retain all three forms as currently used.

### 8. `புரட்சி ஜனநாயகக் கட்சி`

The historical entity remains **Radical Democratic Party**.

**Action:** retain the normalized entity name while preserving Kalaignar's explicit **1939** date exactly as authored.

Entity normalization and chronology correction remain separate editorial operations; only the former is permitted here.

### 9. Forward Bloc

`பார்வர்ட் ப்ளாக்` is securely represented as **Forward Bloc**.

**Action:** retain. No explanatory expansion is needed in the memoir prose.

### 10. `கவிக்குயில் சரோஜினி தேவி`

The active praise epithet is already rendered **the poet-songbird Sarojini Devi**.

**Action:** retain the epithet and the source-led name **Sarojini Devi**. Do not replace it with a detached biographical form solely for external standardization.

### 11. Handwritten-journal vocabulary

The source uses `கையெழுத்து ஏடு`, `கையெழுத்துப் பத்திரிகை` and `கையெழுத்துப் பிரதி` across both publication-form and physical-copy senses.

The existing chapter policy correctly distinguishes:

- **handwritten journal** — the publication form;
- **copy / copies** — individual physical exemplars.

**Action:** retain. This distinction is central to the fifty-copy production account.

### 12. `சிறுதுளி பெருவெள்ளம்`

The compact proverb remains **Little drops make a great flood.**

**Action:** retain as a short image-bearing proverb. Do not replace it with a generalized explanation of cumulative effort.

### 13. `பொங்கல் மலர்`

The publication sense is correctly represented as **Pongal special issue**.

**Action:** retain. `மலர்` is not a literal flower in this context.

### 14. `டெம்மி பேப்பர்`

The period material term remains **demy paper**.

**Action:** retain rather than replacing it with generic “paper” or “printing costs.” The physical production detail is part of the memoir's publishing history.

### 15. `முரசொலி`

**Murasoli** remains visibly a publication title and is not translated semantically.

**Action:** retain **Murasoli**. Its practical origin in this chapter is more important than an explanatory title gloss.

### 16. `துண்டு வெளியீடுகள்`

The current **leaflet issues** preserves both the physical leaflet form and the serial/publication sense.

**Action:** retain **leaflet issues** for this origin passage. Do not substitute generic “pamphlets” if that would weaken the explicit contrast with weekly/monthly publication.

### 17. `பாசறை`

The recruiter describes the Students' Federation as a `பாசறை`.

**Action:** retain **organizing camp**. The movement-camp metaphor remains visible while the English clarifies organizational function.

Do not flatten it to “organization” or “platform.”

### 18. `மாணவர் சம்மேளனம்`

The source gives only a generic Students' Federation and deliberately delays the Communist reveal.

**Action:** retain **Students' Federation** without expanding it to AISF or another specific organization absent source evidence.

This generic treatment is a source-control decision, not a claim that no historical identification could ever be researched outside the memoir.

### 19. `சுதந்திரம் - சமாதானம் - சமத்துவம்`

The triad remains **Freedom – Peace – Equality** in the narrator's later reflection and the equivalent comma-separated wording inside the recruiter's direct speech.

**Action:** retain the same three concepts and order. The dash-separated form should remain visible where the Tamil presents the slogan as a rhythmic triad.

### 20. Final current / depth language

The closing **current** and **depth** are not terminology to be normalized away; they are linked physical images.

**Action:** retain them unchanged through later review gates.
