- `சிறுவர் சீர்திருத்தச் சங்கம்` follows the established **Children's Reform Association** working rendering from v1-ch12; the shortened T1 `Reform Association` is corrected.

- `தமிழ்நாடு தமிழ் மாணவர் மன்றம்` remains **Tamil Nadu Tamil Students' Association**.
- **Dravida Nadu** and **Dravidar Kazhagam** remain established proper-name forms.
- `சுயமரியாதைக்காரன்` is rendered **Self-Respecter**, preserving political identity rather than generic self-respect.
- **Nabi Nayagam festival**, **Mahamayi**, **Palaniyappan**, **Santa**, **Natchuk Koppai** and the Nagai Dravidian Actors' Association remain source-led working forms; no unsupported external normalization is imposed.
- The `ஜன்னி` issue remains closed by the conservative functional phrase **gravely ill patient**, without claiming a modern diagnosis.
