## Review findings so far

### 1. `பெரிய மனிதர்களுக்குத்தான் ... சின்னவர்களுக்குக் கிடையாதா?`

T1 uses “great men” versus “ordinary people”. This conveys meaning but weakens Kalaignar's deliberate `பெரிய` / `சின்ன` antithesis.

**T2 action:** find an English form that preserves the great/small contrast without turning `சின்னவர்கள்` into a contemptuous label. Candidate: “Does life—and history—belong only to the great? Have the small no share in them?” Review aloud before acceptance.

### 2. `உயிர் எமக்கு வெல்லமல்ல`

T1: “life was not so sweet that it must be preserved at the price of submission”.

Problem: this keeps the abstract meaning but drops Kalaignar's concrete `வெல்லம்` (jaggery) image.

**T2 action:** restore the image. Working direction: “Life is no lump of jaggery to us,” followed by English that lets the context carry the idiomatic sense. Add/retain a source note because a literal jaggery image may be unfamiliar to some English readers.

### 3. `சமுதாய நீதிக்கும், சமூகப் புரட்சிக்கும் ஏறக்குறைய கால்கோள் விழா`

T1: “the inaugural ceremony ... had begun for social justice and social revolution”.

Problem: `கால்கோள்` carries a foundation/founding image. “Inaugural ceremony” is smoother but weaker.

**T2 action:** prefer a foundation-laying image, e.g. “as though the foundation stone were being laid for social justice and social revolution,” subject to final cadence review.

### 4. `வெறித்தனமான பக்தி`

T1: “the passionate devotion I bear my leader Anna”.

Problem: “passionate” may soften the deliberately extreme `வெறித்தனமான`.

**T2 action:** test “fierce devotion”, “fervent, almost fanatical devotion”, or another form that preserves intensity without introducing a hostile narrator's judgement.

### 5. `நம்முடைய மாநிலத்திற்கும்`

T1: “between Mysore and our Presidency”.

Problem: “Presidency” is an external historical normalisation not present in the source wording `our state` / `our region-state`.

**T2 action:** return to Kalaignar's wording rather than silently historicalising it. Working form: “between Mysore and our state”.

### 6. 1924 accumulation

T1 intentionally repeats “It was a time when ...” across the historical sweep. This is a positive retention of Kalaignar's repeated `காலம்` construction.

**T2 action:** preserve this build; do not streamline it during English polishing merely for concision.

### 7. Closing Thirukkuvalai image sequence

T1 uses short fragments and exclamations for the temple tank, groves and shrines. This preserves the visual/oral pulse better than converting the passage into one descriptive sentence.

**T2 action:** retain unless T3 finds the English genuinely unreadable.

Continue the full Tamil-English comparison for every rhetorical unit, then update the chapter translation only after the complete voice pass is finished. Do not make isolated stylistic edits without checking their effect on the chapter's overall cadence.
