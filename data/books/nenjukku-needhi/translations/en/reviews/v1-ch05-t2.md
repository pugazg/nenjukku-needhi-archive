## Findings and revisions

### 1. Raja of Bobbili / `முறைப்படி முதல் மந்திரியாக`

The 1932 office remains **First Minister**, consistent with the dyarchy-period terminology already established in `v1-ch04`. The T1 phrase `duly as First Minister` is stiff and does not fully express `பொறுப்பேற்றுக் கொண்டார்`.

**T2 revision:** `the Raja of Bobbili took charge as party leader and formally assumed office as First Minister.`

This preserves both `முறைப்படி` and the assumption-of-office action.

### 2. Ministry-life metaphor

`இந்த மந்திரி சபையின் உயிர் ஏழு ஆண்டுகள் நீடித்தது` personifies the ministry as having a life. T1 correctly preserved that image.

**Action:** retain the `life of the ministry` metaphor; only remove the awkward `served as ministers in between` phrasing around the intervening ministers.

### 3. Sir A. T. Panneerselvam

The Tamil `பன்னீர் செல்வம்` refers to the Justice Party leader commonly rendered in English as **A. T. Panneerselvam**. The T1 form `Pannirselvam` is mechanically transliterated and less useful for historical traceability.

**T2 revision:** normalize to **Sir A. T. Panneerselvam** on first occurrence and **Panneerselvam** thereafter.

Kalaignar's epithets `தஞ்சைத் தங்கம், தமிழ்ச் சிங்கம்` remain visible as **the Gold of Tanjore, the Lion of Tamil**.

### 4. `தலைகீழ்ப் பாடம்`

T1's `could recite it backwards` risks suggesting literal reverse-order recitation. Here the idiom conveys complete mastery/memorisation.

**T2 revision:** `having learnt the history of the Raja of Panagal backwards and forwards`.

This keeps the emphatic idiomatic force without falsely claiming reverse recitation.

### 5. 1937 office vocabulary — Rajaji

The 1937 constitutional setting differs from the earlier dyarchy chapters. Rajagopalachari's period English office was **Premier of the Madras Presidency**. T1's `head of the government in Tamil Nadu` is generic and historically imprecise.

**T2 revision:** use **Premier** for Rajaji in the 1937 ministry passages, while preserving Kalaignar's authored narrative and political wording.

This is institutional normalization, not historical fact-correction.

### 6. `வடமொழி`

In this literary/polemical context, `வடமொழி` is the established Tamil lexical term for **Sanskrit**, not merely an unspecified `northern language`.

**T2 revision:** Maraimalai Adigal's lament begins `Alas! Sanskrit has come and brought Tamil so low!`

The exclamatory lament, `அந்தோ!` / `அய்யகோ!`, remains intact.

### 7. `இறும்பூது எய்துகின்றேன்`

Lexical evidence gives `இறும்பூது` as **wonder / amazement** (with older secondary senses including greatness/magnanimity). T1's `proud wonder` adds a pride component not required by the word itself.

**T2 revision:** `I am filled with wonder.`

The emotional force still comes from the birthday coincidence and Kalaignar's repeated `எண்ணி எண்ணி`; no extra emotion needs to be supplied by the translator.

### 8. Anti-Hindi movement proper names

T2 normalizes several historically attested English forms while retaining Kalaignar's epithets and narrative:

- `பன்னீர் செல்வம்` → **Panneerselvam**;
- `கலிபுல்லா சாகிப்` → **Khalifullah Sahib**;
- `நடராசன்` → **Natarajan** in the historical martyr's name;
- `அகில இந்தியக் காங்கிரஸ் கட்சி` → **Indian National Congress** when naming the historical party.

`Navalar`, `Muthamizh Kaavalar`, `Tamilavel`, `Arignar Anna`, `Perarignar Anna`, and `Ammaiyar` remain culturally/politically visible rather than being stripped away as metadata.

### 9. Dr P. Subbarayan's earlier office

When the chapter looks back to Subbarayan's 1926–1930 government, the historically appropriate dyarchy title is **First Minister**, as established for that period in `v1-ch04`.

**T2 revision:** `Dr Subbarayan, who had governed the Madras Presidency as First Minister with the support of the Justice Party...`

His 1938 role remains **Education Minister** in Rajaji's ministry.

### 10. Thalamuthu–Natarajan blood-and-flag image

T1 correctly preserves `தமிழன்` as a collective personification and does not collapse the passage into the abstract statement that the two men became martyrs.

**Action:** retain the prison-death bluntness and the image of the language-struggle flag dipped in their blood and flown sky-high.

### 11. Voltaire quotation

The T1 now correctly says `As Voltaire said` and freshly translates Kalaignar's Tamil wording. T2 does not import the familiar canonical English maxim and does not fact-correct the attribution inside the memoir.

**Action:** retain the Tamil-mediated quotation and the comparison to Bose's disagreement with Gandhi coupled with fierce defence of Gandhi's welfare, fame, service and honour.

### 12. `தமிழர் தலைவர்`

The phrase first functions as Periyar's public epithet and immediately recurs as the title of Sami Chidambaranar's biography. T1's `Leader of the Tamils` / `Tamil Leader` split weakens that deliberate lexical echo.

**T2 revision:** use **Leader of the Tamils** for both the epithet and the translated book title.

The anomalous source verb `நீட்டினார்` remains disclosed; English states only the secure contextual function that Chidambaranar wrote the biography.

### 13. Anna's closing chorus — `ஆணித்தரமான வாதங்கள்`

T1's `Arguments driven home like nails` creates a nail image that the lexicalized adjective does not require. `ஆணித்தரம்` means firmness/strength/standard; the crowd is praising the firmness of Anna's arguments, not explicitly describing nails being driven.

**T2 revision:** `Firm, forceful arguments.`

The rest of the chorus remains fragmentary and oral: music, restrained stage movement, pomegranate pearls, the opened sluice, Courtallam waterfall, and Tamil `chindu` all remain concrete images rather than being explained away.

### 14. Historical assertions remain authored claims

The source explicitly places the Justice Party's collapse in a 1936 election. External histories commonly describe the decisive provincial election as 1937, but T2 does **not** alter Kalaignar's clear text. Likewise, the chapter's statements about Nehru, Hitler, Stalin, Bose, Gandhi and world events remain translated as authored.

External evidence is used only for lexical/proper-name/institutional normalization.
