## Decisions

- **Kudi Arasu** follows the established publication-title policy and remains untranslated.
- **Periyar** remains the living political/personal name; **Ayya** is retained as the respectful address in direct speech.
- The disguise terms **kaili**, **jippa** and **kullai** are retained with minimal clothing glosses; they are not replaced by stereotyped or invented cultural description.
- **tiffin** is retained for the meal context; the exact salary arithmetic remains 40 − 20 − 10 − 5 = 5 rupees.
- `அண்ணாமலைக்கு அரோகரா!` remains the working translated article title **Arohara for Annamalai!**, retaining the culturally specific cry `Arohara`.
- `தியாகப் பிரம்ம உற்சவம்` remains **Thyaga Brahma festival**, following the source wording rather than importing an external modern event title.
- **Rajakumari**, A. S. A. Sami and the Jupiter company remain source-led cinema names; no unsupported corporate-title normalization is imposed.
