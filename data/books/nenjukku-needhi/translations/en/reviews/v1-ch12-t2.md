1. **Murasoli production term** — `துண்டு அறிக்கைகள்` is aligned with the established glossary as **Murasoli leaflet issues**, not generic leaflets.
2. **`பூரிதட்சிணை` lexical repair** — T1's **feast of religious merit** was not supported by the term. Lexical evidence identifies `பூரிதட்சிணை` as festive monetary dakshina to Brahmans, with `பூரி` carrying abundance. T2 uses the source-compatible exclamation **Bountiful dakshina!**
3. **Conch image** — `சமதர்ம சங்க நாதம்` is restored as the **conch-blast of equality** sounded by Self-Respecters, rather than a generic trumpet-call.
4. **Eat-and-roll sarcasm** — `வீட்டிலே உண்டு உருண்டு கிடக்கும்` now preserves the taunt: the lofty thinkers **eat their fill and roll about at home**.
5. **Gullibility attack** — `இளித்தவாயர்கள்` is sharpened from neutral **simpletons** to **gullible fools** in the polemical register.
6. **Corpse / wedding / money image** — `பிணத்திற்கு மணக்கோலமிட்டு, மக்களிடம் காசு பறிக்கும்` is rendered as dressing the corpse **in wedding finery** and **wringing money from the people**, avoiding the unsupported gender-specific `bridegroom`.
7. **Leaflet climax** — the final Varnashrama paragraph is rebalanced so the ancient-Tamil-land, shameless-self-interest, contortion, death and honour images retain their escalating oratorical force.
8. **Public-speaking transition** — the stiff T1 opening is recast as Kalaignar **cannot help recalling** his first school debate when thinking about how he prepared for public speaking.
9. **Karna's response** — `பூரித்துப் போகிறான்` becomes **overwhelmed by the greatness of his friend's heart**, preserving the emotional function without a literal English oddity.
10. **Retrospective Karna joke** — `இழுக்கு மிகுந்த ஒரு பண்பு` is rendered as a **deeply disgraceful trait**, keeping the older narrator's comic moral re-reading of the youthful friendship speech.
11. **Kural / friendship syntax** — the two friendship quotations are restructured so the corrective advice and distress-removing friends are grammatically clear while the source fragments remain visible as quotations.
12. **Children's-association meeting culture** — the street elder is placed in **our street**; `சுண்டல் கடலை` becomes **chickpea sundal**, preserving the cultural food without T1's duplicate `sundal and chickpeas`.
