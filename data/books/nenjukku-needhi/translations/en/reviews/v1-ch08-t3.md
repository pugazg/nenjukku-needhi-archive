## Accepted revisions

### 1. Growing political dominance

T2:
> there were signs that Congress-Communist dominance was beginning to rise within the Federation

T3:
> **there were signs of growing Congress-Communist dominance within the Federation**

The political force remains unchanged; the English is less mechanical.

### 2. Restore the short dawn beat

Tamil contains a separate `விடிந்தது` after the sleepless night.

T2 compressed this into **At daybreak**.

T3:
> **I did not sleep all night. Dawn came. I went straight...**

This restores Kalaignar's short narrative beat rather than adding content.

### 3. Membership-fee English

T2:
> He had one hundred rupees collected as membership fees.

T3:
> **He had one hundred rupees in membership fees.**

No arithmetic changes.

### 4. Seventy-five-rupee rhetorical cadence

T2:
> I still had seventy-five rupees, did I not?

T3:
> **I still had seventy-five rupees, didn't I?**

The source's `அல்லவா?` remains rhetorical.

### 5. The vanished organizer

T2:
> never appeared before my eyes again

T3:
> **I never saw again the comrade who had thought of trapping me in his net.**

The net image remains; only the English idiom changes.

### 6. District phrasing

T2:
> in Thanjavur district and in other districts

T3:
> **in Thanjavur and other districts**

No geographical scope changes.

### 7. Remove “did not come / not coming” repetition

T2:
> Most ... did not come. Only when ... did I learn that they were not coming.

T3:
> **Most ... did not come. I learned this only when I went to the railway station to receive them.**

The source event and delayed discovery remain intact.

### 8. Absent invitees

T2:
> those who had agreed to come but failed to do so

T3:
> **those who had agreed to come and then stayed away**

This is smoother English for the same source action.

### 9. Mother's insistence

T2:
> My mother had one stubborn wish

T3:
> **My mother was adamant about one thing**

The force of `ஒரே பிடிவாதம்` is retained.

### 10. Fifth/sixth-standard sentence

T2:
> Until the fifth or sixth standard I had even gone to high school with my hair braided

T3:
> **Even into the fifth or sixth standard, I had gone to high school with my hair braided**

The unusual autobiographical detail remains exactly visible.

### 11. Wearing the chain for Mother

T2:
> Still, my mother wanted the pleasure of seeing it on me, and so I had worn it.

T3:
> **But Mother wanted the pleasure of seeing it on me, so I wore it for her.**

This keeps the affectionate motive and improves tense/cadence.

### 12. Broken-link storage

T2:
> had been kept in my mother's box to be taken for repair

T3:
> **had been put away in my mother's box until it could be repaired**

The source circumstance remains unchanged.

### 13. The open box

T2:
> the box stood unlocked

Tamil says the box was open.

T3:
> **The box was open, as though it had been left that way just for me.**

This is both smoother and closer to the source.

### 14. Baby-snake image

T2:
> the gold chain lay curled there like a baby snake newly emerged from an egg

T3:
> **And there lay the gold chain, curled like a baby snake fresh from the egg.**

The concrete image is preserved rather than paraphrased.

### 15. Comic reasoning after Mother's curse

T2:
> I took courage from one thought:

T3:
> **One thought gave me courage:**

The comic logic that follows—everyone turns to dust eventually, and Mother set no deadline—remains untouched.

### 16. Mother's later reaction

T2:
> she was even pleased

T3:
> **she was pleased as well**

This keeps the source's `மகிச்சியும்` without an unintended contrastive “even.”

### 17. Approval visible on her face

T2:
> The approval rising in her heart showed itself on her face

T3:
> **The approval in her heart showed on her face**

The emotional point remains; the sentence loses unnecessary abstraction.

### 18. Sanarthanam's return fare

T2:
> I had sent him the fare to come—but what was I to do about his return?

T3:
> **I had sent him the fare to come. But how was I to send him back?**

The practical problem becomes more immediate, matching the source's short question.

### 19. Closing inward grief

Two small linked cadence corrections were accepted in the final movement:

- **He did.** → **He stayed.**
- **I would weep inside myself.** → **I would weep inwardly.**

The wife's suffering and Kalaignar's silence remain the chapter's closing moral pressure.
