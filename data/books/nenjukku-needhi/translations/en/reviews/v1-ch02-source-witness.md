# v1-ch02 — Source-Witness Decision for the Long `ஏலேலோ` Song

Status: **TEXT STABLE / SEMANTIC INTERPRETATION HOLD**

Tamil source: `pugazg/kalaignar-autobiography/public/data/text/v1-ch02.json` at pinned read-only ref `5c6b5ef8901044660e607d4649238d7c66cb648d`.

Secondary page-level witness: `pugazg/nenjukku-needhi-archive/data/books/nenjukku-needhi/cleaned/volume1/0024.md` on `main`.

## Purpose

T2 left two unresolved expressions in Muthuvelar's long `ஏலேலோ` satire:

1. `தன் வீட்டுத் தரகுமூட்டை / தலைமேலிருந்தும்`
2. `அவசரமாய் முந்தானையால் / கூட்டியே நின்று`

The next activity was to seek a stronger printed/image witness rather than guess from context.

## Witness result

The pinned chapter extraction and the separately retained page-level text witness agree on the visible letter-sequences:

- `தரகுமூட்டை`
- `முந்தானையால் கூட்டியே நின்று`

The public catalogue records for printed/digitised editions were checked, but a trustworthy page-image rendering of the relevant page could not be inspected through the available interface in this review. Therefore this review **does not claim visual verification of the printed glyphs**.

The available evidence nevertheless changes the nature of the hold. The problem is no longer best described as an unreadable or unstable transcription. Across the text witnesses available to this project, the character sequences are stable. What remains uncertain is their exact semantic/syntactic force in this compressed satirical verse.

## Editorial decision

### `தரகுமூட்டை`

Do not restore the earlier T1 paraphrase `burdens of your own household`; that supplied a meaning not securely established by the source.

Until a stronger lexical/printed witness establishes the compound's force, retain the provisional transliteration `taragu-mootai` in the English verse and identify it in translation/source notes.

### `முந்தானையால் கூட்டியே நின்று`

Do not invent an omitted object or narrative action. Retain a conservative rendering that reflects only the recoverable actions (`முந்தானை`, haste, gathering/bringing together, standing) and explicitly mark the syntactic force as unresolved.

## Gate consequence

The two expressions are **semantic interpretation holds**, not character-reading holds.

They are isolated to a quoted song and do not prevent an English-only T3 readability review or a chapter-level thought-structure audit. Those gates may proceed because the uncertainty is transparently delimited and does not obscure the surrounding narrative architecture.

They **do block final `approved` status**. Approval requires either:

- a stronger visual/printed/lexical witness that resolves the meanings; or
- an explicit editorial decision that the documented transliteration/conservative rendering is acceptable as the final scholarly treatment.

No change is made to the read-only source repository.
