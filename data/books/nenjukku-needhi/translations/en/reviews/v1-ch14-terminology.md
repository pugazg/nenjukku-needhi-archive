## Decisions

- `சீர்திருத்தத் திருமணம்` remains transparent **reform marriage** where the source uses that phrase; the later explicitly political `சுயமரியாதை` ceremony remains **Self-Respect marriage**.
- `விவாக சுபமுகூர்த்தம்` remains **vivaha subhamuhurtham** inside the source's contrast with the Self-Respect ceremony.
- **lagna** is retained as the culturally specific auspicious-time concept.
- **Kalaivanar N. S. Krishnan**, **Kinthanar** and **kathakalakshepam** remain source-led performance/name forms.
- `சேரி` is retained as **cheri** but minimally glossed as **cheri settlement** on first use so the social location is not erased or left wholly opaque.
- **Mahamayi**, Padma, Navalar Nedunchezhiyan, Ilamvazhuthi, Vijayaraghavalu and the other personal/place names remain source-led.
