## Comparator findings

### One choice adopted

Tamil: `பெரிய மனிதர்களுக்குத்தான் வாழ்க்கையும் வரலாறும் சொந்தமா? சின்னவர்களுக்குக் கிடையாதா?`

Fresh candidate after T2: “Does life—and history—belong only to the great? Have the small no share in them?”

Existing comparator: “Do life and history belong only to the great? Do the small not own them too?”

**Decision:** adopt the ownership formulation, with the fresh translation's punctuation/rhythm: “Does life—and history—belong only to the great? Do the small not own them too?”

Reason: `சொந்தமா` explicitly frames the question through possession/belonging. “Own them too” preserves that semantic thread while also retaining the deliberate `பெரிய / சின்ன` antithesis. This is a documented post-freeze adoption from the comparator, not benchmark obedience.
