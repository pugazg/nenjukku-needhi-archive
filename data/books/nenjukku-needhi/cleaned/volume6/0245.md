When Jayalalitha's campaign began after filing of nomination in Theni Collectorate on Wednesday; press vehicles were pushed to almost the 100th position in the convoy. This left very little chance for the media to watch the interaction between the AIADMK supreme and the people from whom she was seeking votes.
Times of India photographer K. Sunil Prasad and another lensman from a Tamil paper, managed to reach her by hanging on to Periyakulam MP T.V. Dinakaran's Tata Sumo.
As the convoy stopped at a village and Jayalalitha offered money ranging from Rs. 5 to Rs. 1000 to the people, the Times of India photographer asked her why she was giving away money.
Jayalalitha, who rarely opens up to journalists explained:" You must be a north Indian. This is a custom here. It is a gift we offer as part of the Tamil New Year".
